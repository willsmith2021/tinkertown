The thought behind Tinkertown So many websites offer games for kids, but they come with lots of ads, which aren't good for little ones. So I built Tinkertown for my kid, Manik: a safe place to play and learn, with no ads at all. Being an AI expert, I make things like this for fun, and this game took me about 3 hours to build. I'll keep adding new stages to make it even more exciting for kids!
